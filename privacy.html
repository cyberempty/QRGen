Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Privacy

QRGen does not collect or store your data. Everything you type — links, text, phone numbers, Wi-Fi or contact details — is processed entirely in your browser to generate the QR code. No information is sent to a server, saved in a database, or shared with third parties.

The QR code and any images you download or copy stay only on your device.

External services. The page loads two resources from external providers, needed for the site to work:

  • Google Fonts, for the interface typeface;
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare), for the library that generates QR codes.

Loading these resources may involve a standard technical request (such as your IP address) handled directly by those providers, under their own privacy policies. QRGen has no access to this data and does not use it in any way.

Cookies and local storage. QRGen does not use tracking or profiling cookies, nor any analytics tools.
